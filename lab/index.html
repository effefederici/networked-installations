LABORATORY OF UNREADABLE PHENOMENA / CLOSED ROOM

—

CONNECTING

The room is closed.

Nothing is on show at the moment.

Admission by request.

Leave one word at the door. The doorkeeper reads it and decides who enters. Seats are few; nothing is recorded.

Awaiting admission.

Your word has reached the doorkeeper: . Keep this page open; it opens by itself.

The door stays closed.

The doorkeeper has not admitted this request.

The instrument cannot connect.
