One Letter Short of, 2026
Networked installation: browser, a field of forces, a finite number of molecules.

A concept is a field that organises a sign. In this tank a field of forces acts on a finite number of molecules and draws them towards the six letters of the word UTOPIA. The field is complete: it holds a place for every letter. The matter is not: the tank contains fewer molecules than the word requires. The concept cannot find enough matter to produce the sign that represents it.

At first the molecules move at random. Then the field appears, the cloud is drawn towards the word, and five letters condense, each surrounded by molecules that never settle. The sixth stays empty, and the field pulls hardest towards the empty letter. The only way to fill that letter is to empty another one: a letter gives way, its molecules return to vapour, and only then do they gather where the word was incomplete.

The word is never present as matter. It is present only as reading: the eye restores the missing letter, and UTOPIA is read in a tank that has never contained it. Total equilibrium is the state the tank is built for, and the one state it cannot reach. The shortage is part of the system, and so is the unrest.
