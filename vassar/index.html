Federico Federici

One Letter Short of

Year
2026
Medium
Networked installation: browser, JavaScript, HTML canvas
Dimensions
Variable; full screen, on computers, tablets and phones

A field of forces draws a finite number of molecules towards the six letters of the word UTOPIA. The tank holds fewer molecules than the word requires, so one letter is always missing: the only way to fill that letter is to empty another one. The word is never present as matter, only as reading, since the eye restores the missing letter. The visitor's hand, or finger, acts as a second field and opens a gap in the mist.

A Copy for a Century

Year
2026
Medium
Networked installation: browser, JavaScript, Web Audio, server-side news feeds
Dimensions
Variable; full screen, with sound

A virtual typewriter copies, in real time, the public news feeds of papers in fourteen languages, cuts the headlines into shreds and splices them into a single stream at the edge of noise. Each visitor starts a century of their own, which no reader will see completed. Every character is sonified in the browser: its pitch comes from its Unicode address, the force of its strike from its information in Shannon's sense.
